# Third-party notices: clubs-page card portal

The portal technique in `CardPortalScene.tsx` (a card-shaped drei `MeshPortalMaterial` whose `blend` is eased while the camera moves through it) is adapted from the two MIT-licensed works below. The code here was rewritten for one card; no code is copied verbatim beyond the general approach.

**Nothing else from either project is used.** That includes the 3D models (among them the CC BY-NC airplane), fonts, the Draco decoder and any images. The miniature pitch is built from simple procedural shapes, and the card art is Emblem's own synthetic hero image.

React Three Fiber, drei and three.js are existing dependencies of this project, under their own licences.
