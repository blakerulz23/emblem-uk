# DPO Decision Record — Emblem (Lauda Cartoons Ltd)

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 24 August 2026
**Status:** Controlled draft, recording a decision, not a legal determination. **REQUIRES INDEPENDENT REVIEW** before it can be relied upon.

## Decision

Lauda Cartoons Ltd has not appointed a statutory Data Protection Officer (DPO). Blake Ugo-Ogbonna acts as interim Privacy Lead for the pilot.

## Reasoning offered (for specialist review, not asserted as settled)

Under UK GDPR Article 37, a DPO is mandatory where: (a) processing is carried out by a public authority; (b) the core activities require regular and systematic monitoring of data subjects on a large scale; or (c) the core activities consist of large-scale processing of special category data or data relating to criminal convictions.

Lauda Cartoons Ltd is not a public authority. The current pilot's scale — 2–3 manually approved clubs, one team initially, no more than 30 real players, 8–12 weeks — is deliberately small. Whether this remains true, and whether "regular and systematic monitoring" or "large scale" thresholds are met even at pilot scale (children's data carries heightened regulatory sensitivity regardless of volume), is a judgement this document does not make. **This reasoning is offered for an independent UK data-protection specialist to test, not as Emblem's final legal position.**

## What this decision does not do

- It does not conclude Emblem is exempt from appointing a DPO at a larger scale.
- It does not substitute for the independent Safeguarding Lead the founder has already identified as still needing to be named (see the [founder decision register](./founder-decision-register-v1.md), item G3).
- It does not resolve the conflict-of-interest concern the original DPIA itself already raised: Blake, as the person deciding whether the product is safe, is also the person self-assigned to review that decision. That concern is unchanged by this record.

## Review trigger

This decision should be revisited before any pilot expansion beyond the stated scope (more clubs, more players, direct child accounts, or any material change to public sharing, coach access, or AI processing), and in any case as part of the independent review this whole document set is pending.
